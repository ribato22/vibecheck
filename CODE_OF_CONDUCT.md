# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the vibecheck community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

Many people here are new to coding and building with AI. Questions that seem basic are welcome. Nobody should feel embarrassed for asking how to keep their app safe.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners and people writing in a second language
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address, email, **or API keys and secrets found in their code**, without explicit permission
- Mocking someone's app, code or skill level, including "you shouldn't ship if you can't code"
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsible disclosure

If you find a security problem in someone else's app (for example, an app shared in an issue), don't exploit it, don't publish the details, and tell the owner privately. For problems in vibecheck itself, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions), and also when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [ribato22](https://github.com/ribato22), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. For anything involving leaked secrets or a vulnerability, use the [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/ribato22/vibecheck/security/advisories/new). All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction**: for inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior. A private, written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**: for a violation through a single incident or a series of actions. A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban**: for a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior. A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban**: for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people. A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
